Getting the most out of it

  1. Play the first round together. Sit with them for their first game — you’ll learn the worlds fast, and they’ll love showing you.
  2. Talk about Safe Chat. Explain why it stops phone numbers and “add me on…” invites, so they know to tell you if anyone asks.
  3. Approve friends they know. Kids they know from school or family are the friends worth saying yes to.
  4. Check in weekly. A quick look at the week’s play time and messages keeps everyone on the same page.
