Questions parents ask
My child already plays another block game. Will they like {{ brand }}?

It’s built in the blocky style they already know: design a character, collect hats, faces and pets, earn badges, and jump into worlds — races, mysteries, team games and more. What’s different is the part you see: friends wait for your approval, Safe Chat guards what’s said, and you set the limits.

Is it really free?

Yes. Your family account and your children’s accounts cost nothing, and nothing on {{ brand }} can be bought with real money — Noogets are play money. There’s no credit card to enter.

Can strangers talk to my child?

Only in the ways you allow. Private messages are friends-only by default, Safe Chat stops personal details and “add me on…” invites in both directions, and you can have every new friend wait for your approval. In a game round, other players can see what your child types in the round’s chat — filtered by Safe Chat — and you can switch in-game chat off completely.

Can they still play with their real friends?

Yes. Their friends sign up (it’s free) and send a friend request, and your child accepts it — or, with friend approval switched on, it lands on your dashboard for your OK first. Then they can play and chat together.

What will I see on my dashboard?

What they’re doing right now, play time by day and by hour, each game session, the worlds they play most, their friends and friend requests, their private messages and profile comments, anything Safe Chat stepped in on, their play-money balance and the badges they’ve earned.

Do I need my child there to set it up?

For the second step, it’s best if they are. You’ll choose their username and a password they’ll remember together, and they get to design their character. If they’re not around, create your account now and finish later — it’ll be waiting on your dashboard.

How old should my child be?

{{ brand }} is made for school-age kids, and you decide when yours is ready. Tell us their birth year and we’ll suggest age-appropriate starting rules — gentler worlds for younger children, and no crates until 13 — and you can add daily limits, quiet hours and friend approval, or change anything, any time.

What devices does it work on?

The site and your dashboard work on phones, tablets and computers. The games themselves run in the browser on a computer with a keyboard and mouse — there’s nothing to download or install.

Can I change the rules, or delete the account later?

Any time. Every rule changes in a tap, pausing an account signs your child out everywhere at once, and you can delete a child’s account — or your whole family account — for good.
