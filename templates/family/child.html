How Safe Chat works

  • Stops phone numbers, emails and home addresses — even spelled-out numbers.
  • Stops “add me on…” invites to other apps, and links.
  • Stops requests to meet up, for photos, or to keep secrets.
  • Works both ways: what {{ child.first_name }} sends, and what’s sent to them.
  • Tells you gently if {{ child.first_name }} writes something that can be a sign of feeling low.
